> There are many things we can do to learn English well.

# 🔴 批改详解

| 原句错误 | 错误类型 | 修改建议 |
|---|---|---|
| There are a lot of **advice** | advice不可数，且此句为题目已给首句，不应重写 | 删去，直接用题目给的首句 |
| most of **students** | 冠词缺失 | → most of **the** students |
| **being interviewed** | 语态冗余，表达别扭 | → **surveyed** 或 **who were surveyed** |
| **percent of 65%** | 语序错误 | → **65%** 或 **65 percent** |
| choose to **listen English song** | 介词缺失，名词单复数错误 | → listen to English **songs** |
| will choose **read** | 动词形式错误 | → will choose **to read** |
| **lessones** | 拼写错误 | → **lessons** |
| improve **themselves** English level | 代词格错误 | → improve **their** English level |
| **if you can finish those tips** | 逻辑混乱，"finish tips"搭配错误 | → **by following these suggestions** |
| **for it can help...** | 句子结构断裂，连接词误用 | → 删去"for"，重新组织句子 |
| 图表数据描述**不完整** | 内容缺失 | 18%读书、12%浏览网站均未提及 |

# ✅ 修改后范文（供参考）

**Learning English Beyond the Classroom**

There are many things we can do to learn English well. According to a recent survey on how students learn English outside the classroom, 65% of students choose to listen to English songs, and 50% prefer watching English movies. Meanwhile, 18% enjoy reading English books, while only 12% browse English learning websites.

In my opinion, all of these are effective ways to improve our English. However, I would suggest that students spend more time reading English books, as it helps build vocabulary and develop a deeper understanding of the language. Combining reading with listening to songs and watching movies would make learning both enjoyable and productive.

# 💡 总体建议

- **图表要全面**：四项数据（18%、65%、50%、12%）都应提及，不能只写两项
- **注意介词搭配**：listen **to**, choose **to do**
- **句子逻辑要完整**：避免出现"if…"没有结果句的残缺句
- **建议部分要具体**：说明为什么推荐某种方式，理由要充分
- **词数控制在100词左右**，范文已符合要求
