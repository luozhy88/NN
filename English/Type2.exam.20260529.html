01 题目原文 (The Prompt)

阅读下面材料，根据其内容和所给段落开头语续写两段，使之构成一篇完整的短文。

When Rosa was still a young girl, her parents decided to send her from their home in Tehran to live with her aunt in London, England. The journey was long, and as she flew to England, Rosa spent much of the time imagining what her new life would be like. She was determined to do well at school and make her parents proud. However, when she arrived at the airport in London, it was not quite as she had imagined at all. Worst of all was the fact that everybody was looking at her with strange eyes. Rosa noticed with surprise that she was the only one wearing a scarf. London proved to be a very strange and unfamiliar place. It rained constantly every day during the first week, and Rosa was unimpressed with what she thought was British summertime. Despite being told that her English was good, she struggled to understand people's accents and slang. The young girl felt disappointed with her new surroundings and she missed her family and friends dearly.

When the day came for Rosa to go to her new school, she was extremely nervous. Her aunt tried to comfort her, saying "I'm sure you'll make lots of friends today. You just wait and see." But things didn't go as smoothly as her aunt had hoped, at least initially. Some girls in her class pointed and laughed at her, showing no interest in befriending the new girl. Feeling so different from everyone else, Rosa wished she could be back home with her family. During a lunch break, as Rosa sat alone in the corner of the playground, thinking how to escape back to Tehran, a young girl named Emily approached her. She introduced herself, shared her milkshake with Rosa, and said, "I think your scarf looks cool." In return for her kindness, Rosa took out four biscuits from her pocket and shared them with Emily. Soon the two were playing games, running about in the corner of the playground. They exchanged stories — Rosa told Emily all about life in Tehran. Emily told Rosa about all of the cool things she could do in London.

03 语法与拼写错误 (Grammar & Spelling Errors)

你的作文尝试运用了非谓语动词和倒装句等高级结构，但存在较多基础拼写、不规则动词和语法一致性问题，需重点巩固：

第一段

  • 📄 原句： Wearing a smile on Emily's face, she introducted Rosa to her friends.Rse Rosa breaked into a jourful smile...
    ❌ 错误： introducted / Rse / breaked / jourful
    ✔️ 改正： introduced / As / broke / joyful

    📝 解析：① introducted 拼写错误，应为 introduced。② Rse 应为 As。③ breaked 是不规则动词错误，break 的过去式为 broke。④ jourful 拼写错误，应为 joyful。另外，"Wearing a smile on Emily's face" 表达生硬，建议改为 With a smile on her face。

  • 📄 原句： Time passing by, things began to turn around.
    ❌ 错误： Time passing by
    ✔️ 改正： As time went by

    📝 解析：独立主格 "Time passing by" 虽然语法上可能成立，但在此处显得生硬，与后文衔接不畅。用 As time went by 引导时间状语从句更加自然流畅。

  • 📄 原句： her eyes shining with mounting deternation and confidence.
    ❌ 错误： deternation
    ✔️ 改正： determination

    📝 解析：拼写错误。determination 意为"决心"，是高中核心词汇。

第二段

  • 📄 原句： Rose keeped her's head down, hiding her's features.
    ❌ 错误： Rose / keeped / her's / hiding her's features
    ✔️ 改正： Rosa kept her head down, feeling a bit shy.

    📝 解析：① 主人公名为 Rosa，非 Rose，全文需统一。② keep 的过去式是 kept，不是 keeped。③ her 已是形容词性物主代词，不需要加 's。④ hiding her features 与原文设定不符——Rosa 是因戴头巾而显得与众不同，并非面部有缺陷，不宜臆测添加原文没有的设定。

  • 📄 原句： Fixing Emily's eyes on Rose's face, she encouraged Rose to relax herself.
    ❌ 错误： Fixing Emily's eyes ... she encouraged Rose to relax herself
    ✔️ 改正： Fixing her eyes on Rosa's face, Emily encouraged her to relax.

    📝 解析：① 分词短语置于句首时，其逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致。"Fixing her eyes..." 的动作发出者是 Emily，所以主句主语应为 Emily。原句 "Fixing Emily's eyes" 变成 Emily 把自己的眼睛固定在对方脸上，逻辑怪异。② relax herself 属于中式英语，relax 作不及物动词即可，或说 calm down。

  • 📄 原句： They spread out a blanket under a big tree.anc shared food. Only then did Rose, realized Lodon is a kindful place.
    ❌ 错误： anc / Only then did Rose, realized / Lodon / kindful
    ✔️ 改正： and shared food. Only then did Rosa realize London was such a wonderful place.

    📝 解析：① anc 应为 and。② Only then 置于句首需部分倒装：助动词 did + 主语 + 动词原形。原句用了 realized（过去式）且多了逗号，属双重错误。③ Lodon 应为 London。④ kindful 不是正确的英文单词，应改为 kind, wonderful 或 lovely。

  • 📄 原句： Never before had Rose gut such a unique experience,which was engraved in Rose's mind and had a pround influnence on Rose in the rest of her life.
    ❌ 错误： gut / pround / influnence / in the rest of her life
    ✔️ 改正： got / profound / influence / for the rest of her life

    📝 解析：① gut 应为 got（拼写错误）。② pround 应为 profound。③ influnence 应为 influence。④ in the rest of her life 介词搭配错误，应为 for the rest of her life。另外，连续使用 Rose 显得重复，可用代词 she/her 替换。

05 修改建议 (Suggestions for Improvement)

  1. 夯实基础词汇与不规则动词： 这是目前最急需解决的问题。break-broke-broken, keep-kept-kept 等不规则动词必须熟记；常见词汇如 introduce, joyful, determination, profound, influence 的拼写要反复强化，考场上建议留2分钟专门"排雷"。
  2. 严守分词逻辑主语一致原则： 每次使用 "Doing..., sb. did..." 结构时，务必确认分词的动作发出者就是主句的主语。如 "Fixing her eyes on Rosa's face, Emily encouraged..." 中，Fixing 的动作发出者是 Emily，主句主语也必须是 Emily。
  3. 掌握倒装句的准确结构： Only then did + 主语 + 动词原形；Never before had + 主语 + 过去分词。助动词 did/had 已经体现了过去时态，后面的实义动词必须用原形/过去分词，不能再用过去式。
  4. 忠于原文设定，合理推演情节： 原文中 Rosa 的"不同"在于她戴了头巾（scarf），并非面部有缺陷。续写不能脱离原文随意添加设定。第二段中 Rosa 在野餐时可以是略带害羞，但不应该是 "hiding her features"。
  5. 减少模板化套话，增加真实细节： 结尾 "engraved in my mind and had a profound influence" 套话痕迹较重。可以加入更多感官细节（阳光、食物的香味、笑声）来自然烘托情感，让升华水到渠成。
