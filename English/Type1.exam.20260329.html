3 作文批改与语法解析

总体来说，你的作文思路清晰，完美地覆盖了题目要求的三个要点（分享新鲜事、表达感受、询问对方），而且使用了一些很棒的词汇（如 local specialties, explore the unknown）。但在语法、拼写和标点上存在一些小瑕疵，具体批改如下：

1. 介词与搭配

原句： I'm glad to tell you my special holiday experience.

修改： I'm glad to tell you about my special holiday experience.

解析： “告诉某人关于某事”通常用 tell sb. about sth.。

2. 冠词缺失

原句： The most exciting news is that I traveled to sea with my family!

修改： The most exciting thing is that I traveled to the sea with my family!

解析： 去海边应该用 travel to the sea 或 travel to the seaside。另外，用 thing (事情) 或者 part (部分) 比用 news (新闻) 更自然。

3. 句子结构不完整（无谓语）

原句： The blue sky and sea.

修改： We enjoyed the beautiful blue sky and sea. （或合并到上一句）

解析： 英语中一个完整的句子必须要有主语和谓语动词。"The blue sky and sea." 只是一个名词短语，缺乏动词，构不成完整的句子。

4. 大小写与格式

原句： we also tried local specialties like Russian- style bread.

修改： We also tried local specialties like Russian-style bread.

解析： 句首字母必须大写，所以 "we" 要改为 "We"。另外，连字符两边通常不加空格，应写为 "Russian-style"。

5. 时态问题

原句： This trip gives me many unforgettable memories.

修改： This trip gave me many unforgettable memories.

解析： 这次旅行发生在刚刚过去的寒假，动作已经完成，所以谓语动词应该用一般过去时 gave。

6. 拼写与标点符号

原句： Have you done anything exciting during your holioay.

修改： Have you done anything exciting during your holiday?

解析： holiday 拼写错误。同时，这是一个疑问句，句末必须使用问号 ?。

✨ 修改后的完美版本：

I'm glad to tell you about my special holiday experience. The most exciting thing is that I traveled to the sea with my family! We enjoyed the beautiful blue sky and sea. We also tried local specialties like Russian-style bread. This trip gave me many unforgettable memories. I now feel braver to explore the unknown. Have you done anything exciting during your holiday?

4 参考范文

Dear Tom,

How is everything going?

I am writing to share a special experience from my winter vacation. I went to Harbin with my family and saw the breathtaking ice sculptures for the first time! We also tried skiing, which was extremely challenging but thrilling. Standing on the snow, I felt like I was in a fairy-tale world. I am so proud of myself for stepping out of my comfort zone.

Did you experience anything interesting during your winter holiday?

I'm looking forward to your reply.

Yours,

Li Hua

5 学习建议

  • 注意句子的完整性（避免残句）： 每次写完一个句子（以句号结束），都要检查一下：这个句子里有没有动词？比如你的 "The blue sky and sea." 就是典型的残句。
  • 检查基本功（拼写、大小写、标点）： 考试中，单词拼写错误（如 holioay）、句首未大写、疑问句没加问号，这些都是容易避免的“冤枉分”。建议写完后留出1-2分钟专门检查这些基本细节。
  • 注意时态的一致性： 题目说是“在刚刚过去的寒假中”，所以叙述旅行过程和发生的事情时，一定要牢记使用一般过去时。表达现在受到的影响时（如 I now feel braver）用一般现在时是正确的，这点你做得很好！
  • 积累闪光点： 你文中的 explore the unknown 和 Russian-style bread 非常亮眼，不仅语言高级，而且内容生动具体。继续保持这种增加具体细节的写作习惯，这会让你的文章在众多试卷中脱颖而出！
