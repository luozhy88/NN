高考英语应用文批改报告

一、 展示原文

Dear Grace,

Our school will hold a English Drama Festival. I as the memeber of English Drama club .I am honour to invite you as a host to hold the anctive.

Firstly, the English drama is made for Chinese drama and English translate. Because you are English student and have much experience in hosting activity.

二、 逐句批改

1. Our school will hold a English Drama Festival.
  • ❌ 错误： a English
  • ✅ 修改： an English
    • 解析：English 的发音以元音音素开头，不定冠词需要用 an。
2. I as the memeber of English Drama club .I am honour to invite you as a host to hold the anctive.
  • ❌ 错误： 拼写错误 (memeber -> member, anctive -> activity/event)。
  • ❌ 错误： 标点与句子结构断裂。“I as the member of...” 不能单独成句。
  • ❌ 错误： 词性误用。honour 是名词，应为 I am honored。
  • ✅ 修改： As a member of the English Drama Club, I am honored to invite you to be the host of this event.
3. Firstly, the English drama is made for Chinese drama and English translate.
  • ❌ 错误： 中式英语较重。translate 这里是动词，不能当名词用。
  • ✅ 修改： The plays performed at the festival are adapted from classic Chinese dramas and translated into English.
    • 解析：表达“由...改编而来”可以用 be adapted from。
4. Because you are English student and have much experience in hosting activity.
  • ❌ 错误： 句子碎片。Because 引导的是从句，不能单独成句。
  • ❌ 错误： 冠词与单复数错误。应为 an English student，activities 应为复数。
  • ✅ 修改： Knowing that you are an English major with rich experience in hosting activities, we believe you are the perfect candidate.

三、 示范性文章

Dear Grace,

I am writing on behalf of the English Drama Club to invite you to be the host of our upcoming English Drama Festival.

The festival, which aims to spark students' interest in English, is scheduled to be held in our school hall next Friday afternoon. Interestingly, all the plays performed at the festival are adapted from classic Chinese dramas and translated into English. Knowing that you are an exchange student from the UK with rich experience in hosting similar activities, we believe your excellent language skills and stage presence will definitely make this event a great success.

We would be truly honored if you could join us. Looking forward to your early reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Hua

四、 针对后续学习的建议

  • 注重基础语法与拼写： 像 a/an 的误用、单复数、词性误用以及拼写错误都是高考阅卷中的“硬伤”，会直接导致降档扣分。建议写完草稿后专门检查这些低级错误。
  • 避免中式英语 (Chinglish)： 尽量积累地道的英语表达，而不是逐字将中文翻译成英文。
  • 注意句子的完整性： 英文句子必须要有主句，不要把 Because 引导的原因状语从句当作一个独立的句子用句号断开。
  • 完善文章结构： 一篇完整的邀请信需要有：表明目的 -> 补充细节 -> 表达期待。原文缺少了结尾的期待和落款。
