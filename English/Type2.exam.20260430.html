Born and raised in China, I've always taken the subtlety of our daily expressions for granted. That was until Mateo, my American classmate and close friend, came to study in China and raised a confusion that made me rethink our culture deeply. Absolutely fascinated by Chinese, Mateo immersed himself in every detail of daily life to learn the language sincerely. Every morning he said "xiexie" at the vending machine and on his way home, he greeted a parrot with "ni hao". Pure, polite and curious, he was trying hard to fit into this new culture.


One afternoon after class, we sat on the campus bench watching the sunset. Mateo's cheerful face suddenly darkened. Frowning, he poured out his long-lasting confusion. "I love Chinese, but there's one thing I just can't understand: your way of speaking," he whispered, puzzled and helpless. "It's too indirect. He continued with obvious frustration, "Last month I bought a bright red scarf for my host mom. She smiled widely, saying 'It's beautiful, thank you!' But she has never worn it. I wanted to explain that our indirectness came from deep consideration. We avoided hurting others and valued harmony. Yet all words stuck in my throat. I simply patted his shoulder and promised I would help him understand. His confusion stuck in my mind, and I determined to find a chance to solve it.


Days passed. Then one weekend, a great opportunity came as we stepped into the old alley near our school-my grandma's favorite place for handmade desserts. We soon lost ourselves in the peaceful atmosphere there. We stopped at a dessert shop run by a warm-hearted owner. My grandma was chatting cheerfully with the shop owner, her best friend.

五、 写作建议 (Suggestions)

💡 1. 深入理解题目“伏笔” (Plot Logic)：
读后续写最重要的是**前后照应**。第一段开头说老板递给我一盒甜点，而前文提到奶奶和老板是好朋友。这时候老板不收钱，或者奶奶不直接表达感谢而是用其他方式，**正是向 Mateo 解释“中国式含蓄”的最佳生活案例**！你的文章中只是简单地“买个甜点送给他”，浪费了前文奶奶和老板这两个人物的出场。要在描写中展示“含蓄的关爱”。

💡 2. 补充具体细节 (Show, Don't Tell)：
第二段你说 “I told Mateo about Chinese indirectness”，但是**到底说了什么？** 怎么解释的？阅卷老师想看到的是你具体的解释内容。比如你可以说：“我指着老板对奶奶的关爱告诉他，我们中国人不喜欢把爱挂在嘴边，而是通过行动（比如多给点心、比如妈妈收起围巾是舍不得戴）来表达。”

💡 3. 夯实语法基础 (Grammar & Punctuation)：
你的文章中出现了严重的**“标点逗号/句号乱用”**问题。非谓语动词（Doing...）和状语从句（Because... / After...）不能用句号单独断开。平时练习要多注意**简单句的完整性**，避免出现残缺句。另外，加强词汇拼写的准确性（dessert, variety, Chinese, succeed）。
