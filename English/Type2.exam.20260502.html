📖 原文阅读

I was in high school before I realized my father had a birth problem. He had a harelip and cleft palate, but to me, he'd looked the same since the day I was born. I still remember kissing him goodnight as a kid and asking if my nose would go flat after a lifetime of kisses. He smiled and assured me it wouldn't, but I remembered a twinkle in his eyes. I was sure he was marveling about a daughter who thought her kisses, not thirty-three operations, had reshaped his face.

My father was kind, patient, thoughtful and loving, my lifelong hero. Never did he let his appearance hold him back. When rejected for sales work for being "too unattractive", he delivered goods by bike and made his own route. When turned away by the army, he became a volunteer. People always accepted his disability calmly when they met his warm smile and positive spirit.

But as a so-called "sophisticated teen", I grew embarrassed by him, barely tolerating being in the same room with the man who'd let me watch him shave every morning for a decade. The man I once admired now seemed hopelessly old-fashioned, especially compared to my trendy friends.

One afternoon, my classmates and I were hanging out at my home, laughing and playing games in the living room. Suddenly, Dad walked in, his usual gentle smile on his face. He was eager to host them, hurrying to the kitchen to pour cold sodas and pop fresh popcorn, chatting softly with my friends as he handed out the snacks. Just then, one of my classmates tugged my sleeve and whispered, "What's wrong with your dad?" His words hit me hard. I felt a sharp sting of shame. I mumbled an excuse, hurried all my classmates out and spoke to Dad in an impatient, cold tone when he looked at me in confusion, my face burning with embarrassment.

构 致命结构与抄写错误

  • 未分段： 读后续写必须严格分为两段。你把所有内容写成了一段。
  • 位置错误： I knew I had to make it right. 必须作为第二段的首句。
  • 抄写错误： 将原题的过去时 knew 抄成了 know。
1 语法错误：缺漏谓语动词

A sense of guilt me when I thought of his kindness over the years.

✨ 推荐：

A sense of guilt overwhelmed / hit / struck me when I thought of his kindness over the years.

解析： guilt（内疚感）是名词，不能直接作动词连接宾语 me。需加上动词如 overwhelmed（淹没）或者 hit/struck（击中），表达“内疚感淹没了我”。
2 拼写与语法缺失

It was then that I realied how ungrateful, taking his love for granted,

✨ 推荐：

It was then that I realized how ungrateful I had been, taking his love for granted.

解析： ① realied -> realized。② how ungrateful 后需补充主谓语构成宾语从句 how ungrateful I had been。③ 句末应为句号。

3 拼写与逻辑漏洞

withont delay, I walked to the kitchen,wrapped my arms around him from behind in his ear.

✨ 推荐：

Without delay, I walked to the kitchen, wrapped my arms around him from behind and whispered in his ear.

解析： ① withont -> without，首字母需大写。② 逻辑缺失：漏掉了动词，必须加上 and whispered (低语) 或描述说了什么。

4 拼写与套话改进

Never before had I got such a unique exerprience, which was deeply engraved in my mind and had a Profound influnence on me.

✨ 推荐：

Never before had I had such a unique experience, which was deeply engraved in my mind and had a profound influence on me.

解析： ① 拼写及大小写：exerprience -> experience; influnence -> influence; Profound 不应大写。② 结尾过于“模板化”，建议扣回“不再为父亲感到羞耻，他永远是英雄”的核心矛盾。

推荐范文与建议

请仔细体会范文是如何严格遵循两段式结构，并在第二段通过语言和动作描写实现情感升华的。

Amid the noise of my classmates leaving, I caught the look of hurt in Dad's eyes. His usual warm smile vanished, replaced by a forced, awkward curve of his lips as he quietly began picking up the half-empty soda cans. Fixing my eyes on his drooping shoulders, I felt my face burning with shame. A heavy wave of guilt overwhelmed me. I remembered his thirty-three operations, his endless patience, and how he had never let his appearance define him. Yet, his own daughter had just treated him like a shameful secret. The sharp contrast between his unconditional love and my selfish vanity made me hate myself.

I knew I had to make it right. Without delay, I walked into the kitchen where he was silently washing the cups. I wrapped my arms around his waist from behind and buried my face in his back. "Dad, I'm so sorry," I choked out, tears streaming down my cheeks. "I was foolish and mean." He paused, dried his hands, and turned around to pull me into a tight embrace. "It's okay, sweetheart," he whispered gently. Looking up at his familiar, scarred face, I saw nothing but beauty and love. From that day on, I never felt embarrassed by my hero again.

🎯 核心提分锦囊

1. 严格遵守格式

读后续写必须且只能是两段，每段开头必须一字不差地抄写给定的段首语。千万不能合并段落或擅自修改时态。

2. 预留时间查拼写

你的词汇量其实不错，但粗心导致了 withont, realied 等低级错误。写完后务必留出2分钟专门做“拼写排雷”。

3. 拒绝套话结尾

类似 "profound influence" 过于空泛。结尾一定要扣回文章的核心矛盾（如：不再为父亲感到羞耻，他是我的英雄）。
