Model Essay

Dear Sophia,

I'm delighted to tell you about a basketball friendly match our team will hold. The game is scheduled to take place in our school stadium on Saturday, October 15th, starting at 2 p.m. So significant is the game that everyone is required to be present.

Please remember to bring your basketball shoes, sports shorts, and a water bottle. Make sure you are well-prepared before the match.

I wonder if you can attend on time. We are counting on you! Please let me know as soon as possible.

I'm looking forward to your reply.

Yours,
Li Hua

💡 学习建议

Suggestions for Improvement

  • 01 牢记邮件/书信格式：称呼（Dear …,）→ 正文 → 结尾礼貌语（Yours, / Best regards,）→ 署名，缺一不可，每项都有分值。
  • 02 审清题目要点：本题明确要求三点内容，写作时可列提纲逐一对应，避免遗漏。比赛信息需写明时间、地点，越具体越好。
  • 03 拼写是基本分：本次有 5 处拼写错误（shoes, required, wonder, p.m., basketball），建议平时注重单词拼写积累，写完后务必通读检查。
  • 04 掌握倒装句型：So + adj. + be + 主语 + that… 是高频句式，that 不可省略。用好此类高级句式可提升得分。
  • 05 地道表达积累：用 be present / attend 替代 show themselves；用 sports shorts 而非 sport-shorts。多读英文范文，培养语感。
