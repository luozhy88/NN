Learning English Beyond the Classroom

There are many things we can do to learn English well. According to a recent survey on how students learn English outside the classroom, 65% of students choose to listen to English songs, and 50% prefer watching English movies. Meanwhile, 18% enjoy reading English books, while only 12% browse English learning websites.


In my opinion, all of these are effective ways to improve our English. However, I would suggest that students spend more time reading English books, as it helps build vocabulary and develop a deeper understanding of the language. Combining reading with listening to songs and watching movies would make learning both enjoyable and productive.

💡 总体建议
📊

图表要全面

四项数据（18%、65%、50%、12%）都应提及，不能只写两项。

🔗

listen to, choose to do，固定搭配需牢记。

避免出现"if…"没有结果句的残缺句，保持句子完整性。
