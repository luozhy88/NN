亲情、成长与接纳

一、 题目说明

故事背景： 作者的父亲患有唇腭裂，但他乐观、善良。小时候作者觉得父亲是英雄，但到了青春期，开始对父亲的外貌感到尴尬。

冲突事件： 同学来家里玩，悄悄问作者“你爸爸怎么了？”，作者感到羞耻，赶走同学并对父亲态度冷淡。

续写任务：

  • 第一段开头： Amid the noise of my classmates leaving, I caught the look of hurt in Dad’s eyes.（重点描写父亲受伤的反应及作者的愧疚）。
  • 第二段开头： I knew I had to make it right.（重点描写道歉、父亲的宽容及积极影响）。

三、 对你答案的详细批改

你的续写在情感走向和情节设计上非常准确，尝试使用了高级词汇和句型（如独立主格结构）。但存在较多拼写错误、语法错误以及语句重复问题。

第一段 - 句 1

原文： ...Dad’s eyes. latching sight of the look of hurt in Dad's eyes amid the noise of my classmates leaving.

点评： 语意严重重复，且拼写有误(catching)。既然给定的首句已表达此意，后半句应直接删除。

第一段 - 句 2

原文： I felt a sharp pang of guitt piercing my heart.

修改： I felt a sharp pang of guilt piercing my heart.

点评： 拼写错误。但句型 a sharp pang of... piercing my heart 用得非常好！

第一段 - 句 3

原文： The science between us heavier than any words, The creak of the floor boards echoing my regret.

修改： The silence between us was heavier than any words, and the creak of the floor boards echoed my regret.

点评： `science` (科学)应为 `silence` (沉默)。缺乏谓语动词，建议改成并列句或规范的独立主格。

第二段 - 句 1

原文： My mind made up to apologize sincerely, my hands trembling signtly with nervousness, I telling him how deeply sorry I was for my nude behavior,

修改： My mind made up to apologize sincerely, and my hands trembling slightly with nervousness, I told him how deeply sorry I was for my rude behavior.

点评： 严重拼写失误：`nude`(裸体的) -> `rude`(粗鲁的)。`telling` 不能作谓语，应改为 `told`。逗号过多导致流水句。

第二段 - 句 2

原文： Dad pulled me into a warm hug, Trom that moment on, which was deeply engraved in my mind and had a profound influence on ne the rest of my life.

修改： Dad pulled me into a warm hug. That moment was deeply engraved in my mind and had a profound influence on me for the rest of my life.

点评： `which` 定语从句用法错误，强行揉在一起。应断句使其表意清晰。

四、 优质范文 (Model Essay)

Amid the noise of my classmates leaving, I caught the look of hurt in Dad’s eyes. The warm smile that always graced his face vanished, replaced by a mixture of confusion and sorrow. He stood rooted to the spot, holding a tray of untouched popcorn. At that moment, a sharp pang of guilt pierced my heart. The silence between us was heavier than any words, making me realize how deeply my cold attitude had wounded the man who loved me most. Silently, he placed the snacks on the table and retreated to his room. Looking at his slightly hunched back, my face burned not with embarrassment, but with profound shame for my own shallowness.

I knew I had to make it right. Later that evening, with my hands trembling slightly out of nervousness, I hesitantly knocked on his door. Taking a deep breath, I walked up to him and choked out a sincere apology, tears welling up in my eyes. I told him how deeply sorry I was for my rude behavior and reassured him that he would always be my lifelong hero. Without a word of blame, Dad smiled gently and pulled me into a warm, forgiving hug. That moment was deeply engraved in my mind, teaching me that true beauty lies not in a flawless appearance, but in a loving heart.

五、 给你的学习建议

  • 极其注意拼写和笔误

    你的词汇积累不错，但像 nude/rude、science/silence 这种形近词的拼写错误在考试中是致命的，不仅扣分，还会改变句意。平时打字或书写要多复查。

  • 避免句子重复和串段

    给定的首句不要在正文中再抄写一遍。注意段落任务分配：第一段主写情绪（愧疚），第二段再写行动（走向房间道歉），避免第一段把第二段的剧情抢先写完。

  • 攻克长难句的“流水”问题

    尝试高级语法是加分项，但要牢记：一个英语句子里必须且只能有一个主句的谓语动词。长串修饰语写完后，务必检查主谓结构是否完整。

  • 积累升华句型

    结尾的升华 had a profound influence on me 很好。还可以结合主题加入 true beauty lies in a loving heart 这样的点睛之笔，做到首尾呼应。

Keep up the good work! 期待你写出更优秀的文章。
